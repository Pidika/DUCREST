# Ducrest Partners News & Insights guide

The content studio is available at <https://ducrest-partners.sanity.studio/>. Sign in with the Google account invited to the Ducrest Partners Sanity project.

## Create an author

1. Select **Author** in the left menu and select **+**.
2. Enter the person’s name, role and biography, then add a professional photograph when applicable.
3. Select **Publish**.

Create an author before assigning that person to an article. Use the guest-author field only when the writer should not have a reusable profile.

## Publish an article

1. Select **News & Insight**, then select **+**.
2. Enter a clear title and generate the URL slug. Avoid changing the slug after sharing the article publicly.
3. Write a summary of no more than 320 characters.
4. Choose **Thought Leadership**, **Legal Alerts**, or **Events & Media**.
5. Set **Publication status** to **Published** only after legal and editorial review.
6. Set the publication date. A future date keeps the article hidden until that time.
7. Select an author or enter a guest-author name.
8. Upload a featured image and add descriptive alternative text.
9. Add the article body. Use **Heading** for major sections and **Subheading** for subsections.
10. Select every applicable practice area and sector from the predefined lists.
11. Optionally add an SEO title of no more than 60 characters and an SEO description of no more than 160 characters.
12. Select **Publish**. Published changes normally appear on the website within one minute.

## Edit, unpublish or remove content

- Edit an article and select **Publish** again to update it.
- Use **Unpublish** to remove an article publicly while retaining it in the Studio.
- Delete content only when it should be removed permanently.
- Keep an existing slug after publication unless a website administrator has arranged a redirect.

## Editorial and access checks

Confirm names, dates, legal citations and claims against an approved source. Do not publish confidential client details, legal advice for a specific reader, unapproved case outcomes, or images without permission.

A Sanity project administrator invites or removes editors. Each editor should use an individual account. Grant administrator access only to people who need to manage members or project settings.
